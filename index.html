    How the match score works
    • Each factor scores a neighborhood from 0 to 100. Most are percentiles among NYC's residential areas (100 = best in the city).
    • Rent, commute and flood use fixed curves instead: rent against your budget, minutes of commute, the share of land that floods.
    • The match is the average of the factors you turn on, weighted by these shares. Must-haves (bedrooms, budget, commute, boroughs) decide what's listed, not the score.

    Every priority is off, so no neighborhood gets a score. Turn at least one up.
